About

Storytelling, with technology behind it.

BTM Solutions is a creative and technology company. We work in two areas: video and film editing, and AI video creation.

On the editing side, we shape footage into films, trailers, promotional videos and social content, with close attention to pacing, sound and finish. On the AI side, we create AI-generated videos for businesses, brands, content creators, YouTubers, filmmakers, producers, marketing agencies, educators and others who need video content.

We care about getting the details right and about understanding what a client is trying to achieve before we start. Clients in Nigeria and internationally are welcome.

Process

Working with us.

Video & film editing

  1. Project brief

    Understand your goals, footage and requirements.

  2. Planning

    Set the editing direction, deliverables and timeline.

  3. Editing

    Assemble the footage and refine story, pacing, visuals and audio.

  4. Review

    Collect your feedback and make the agreed revisions.

  5. Final delivery

    Prepare and deliver the approved video in the formats you need.

AI video creation

  1. Project brief

    Understand what the video is for, who it is for and where it will be used.

  2. Planning

    Agree the concept, style, length and deliverables.

  3. Creation

    Create the AI-generated video based on the agreed direction.

  4. Review

    Collect your feedback and make the agreed revisions.

  5. Final delivery

    Deliver the approved video in the formats you need.

This is our proposed process. We adjust it to suit the size and needs of each project.
